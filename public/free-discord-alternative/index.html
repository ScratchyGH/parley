A free Discord alternative for your friends and community

Parley is a chat app with servers, channels, voice, video, screen share and bots. It costs nothing, shows no ads and does not need an email address.

Start in a minute

Pick a username, save your recovery code, then join a friend's server or make your own. It opens in your browser, and apps are available if you want them.

What you get

  • Servers that fit your group. Channels, roles, threads, forums, stages, polls and scheduled events.
  • Voice, video and screen share. Calls travel straight between people. Noise control, push to talk and live captions are built in.
  • Bots your way. Build one with blocks in Bot Lab or use the HTTP API, and share it through the Bot hub.
  • Big files. Send large files and download them from other members too.
  • Rich links. Links show a card with their picture and title, and itch.io games can be played inside a message.
  • Make it yours. Themes, banners, nameplates and frames, earned with Pars rather than bought.

How Parley compares

Parley covers the everyday things people use a Discord server for: text channels, voice rooms, screen sharing and bots. The difference is the cost model. There is no subscription tier, because calls, screen shares and big files move between members when they can instead of through paid infrastructure.

Parley is built for friend groups and small to mid-sized communities. It is a separate service, so you start a new server and invite people with a link.

Questions

Is Parley really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription and no paid tier. Pars are earned by being around, not bought.

Do I need an email address?

No. You get a recovery code when you sign up. You can add an email later to reset a forgotten password. PMail offers free private addresses that work with it.

Can I use voice, video and screen share?

Yes, in the browser and in the apps.

Does it support bots?

Yes. Build one with blocks or use the HTTP API with a bot token.

Which devices does it run on?

Any modern browser, plus apps for Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iPhone and Ubuntu Touch.
