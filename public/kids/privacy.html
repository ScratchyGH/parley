Parley Kids privacy notice for parents

Version 2026-10-30 (updated for chat). Parley Kids is for children 10 and under. A parent or legal guardian must give consent before any account is made.

What we collect, and when

  • Before your consent: only a random public key and a random device ID that the app generates on the device. Nothing about the child. If a device is never added by a parent, it is deleted after one day.
  • After you consent in PK Connect: the username, display name and avatar the child picks, which you can change or reject before approving.
  • Chat: messages your child sends to friends both parents approved, who their friends are, and a short note whenever a message is blocked or reported. Messages are deleted after 30 days. Messages are checked by an automated filter before they send. Reports are read by Parley staff. There are no images, files or voice.
  • Servers, Pars and the shop: the servers your child makes or joins (only after you approve), their members, and messages in them, which are checked the same way and deleted after 30 days. Pars earned from games and the cosmetic items your child owns and wears. Pars have no money value. We also keep when your child was last online so friends can see if they are around.
  • Parent ID check (optional): if you choose it, a photo of your ID is stored only until a staff member reviews it, then it is deleted. We keep only whether the check passed.
  • Device activity: when a device was last used, and setup events such as codes created and devices revoked. These show in your Activity list and are deleted after 90 days.

We do not collect a child's real name, birthday, email, phone number, address, photos, voice or location. Parley Kids has no advertising, no analytics trackers and no behavioural profiling.

How it is used

Only to run Parley Kids and to let you control it. Children's information is never sold, never shared with advertisers or other third parties, and never used to train models.

Your rights as a parent

  • Review: PK Connect, Account, Download my data gives you everything stored about your family.
  • Refuse further collection: revoke a device and it loses access at once. Delete a child account to remove it.
  • Delete: deleting a child or your parent account removes the related data right away.
  • Withdraw consent: you can do any of the above at any time.

Keeping it safe

Devices prove who they are with a key that stays on the device. Activation codes expire, work once and only on the device they were made for. Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes.

Retention

Account data is kept until you delete it. Chat messages (30 days), rejected accounts (30 days), reports and activity (90 days), unused codes and sessions are cleaned up automatically.

Limits you should know about

Consent today is recorded when a signed in parent confirms it in PK Connect. It is not yet checked with a card, ID or signed form, so do not treat it as a full verification method. The chat filter catches most unsafe messages but no filter is perfect, so please stay involved. The optional ID check shows a parent is an adult but does not prove they are the child's parent.

Contact

Questions or requests: message ParleyOfficial on Parley.
