Voice and video that stay fast

Calls travel straight between people, so they are quick and cost nothing to run. Noise control, push to talk and live captions are built in, and you can join even when your mic is blocked.

Screen share on every device

Share a window or your whole screen. If two people cannot connect directly, other viewers pass the stream along, so it keeps working without a relay server.

Servers that fit your group

Why it costs nothing

Parley runs on free hosting and hands the heavy work to the people using it. Calls, screen shares and big files move between members when they can, so there is almost no server bill to pass on.

That is also why there are no ads and no paid tier. Pars are earned, not bought.

  • Peer to peer firstMedia goes device to device and falls back to other members before any server.

Questions

Is Parley really free?

Yes. It runs on free hosting, has no ads and has no paid plan. Pars, the in-app currency, are earned by using it.

Do I need an email address?

No. You choose a username and get a recovery code. Keep the code somewhere safe, because it is the only way back into your account.

How do I get it on my iPhone?

Open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. You can also tap Profile under iPhone and iPad, allow it, and finish in Settings.

Why does my computer warn me about the installer?

The Windows and Mac apps are not signed with a paid certificate. They are built on GitHub from this repository, and the Linux AppImage and packages are built the same way.

Can I upload programs and files?

Yes. Anyone downloading a program file such as an .exe has to confirm they trust it first. Parley cannot scan it, so only download programs from people you know.

Do the apps get updates?

The apps open the live site, so the app itself is always current. The Windows and Linux apps also update their wrapper in the background.

Do I have to install anything?

No. Open in browser works anywhere, and your account is the same on every device.
