Parley

Chat, call and hang out. As an app.

Servers, voice, screen share and games in one free app. It opens in its own window, takes almost no storage and updates itself.

Open web version

Everything a community needs

Voice and video

Peer to peer calls with noise control, push to talk and captions. Joins even when your mic is blocked.

Screen share on every device

Share a window or your whole screen. If two people cannot connect directly, other viewers pass the stream along, so it works without a relay server.

Servers and channels

Roles, threads, forums, stages, polls and scheduled events.

Play inside chat

Post an itch.io link to a browser game and play it right in the message.

Hollow games

Gourd Rush, Terror Night and a horror shooter, on desktop and touch.

Pars and cosmetics

Earn Pars, collect banners, nameplates and seasonal sets.

Light on your device

No big download. Installs as a small web app and keeps itself current.

Private by design

No email needed. Your recovery code is the only key to your account.

Questions

Is Parley free?

Yes. It runs on free hosting and has no ads.

How do I get it on my iPhone?

Open this page in Safari and tap iPhone and iPad under Downloads, then allow the profile and install it from Settings. You can also use Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Why does my computer warn me about the installer?

The Windows and Mac apps are not signed with a paid certificate yet. The installer is built in the open on GitHub from this repository.

Do I have to install it?

No. Open web version works in any modern browser.

Installed Parley is the full app. Nothing is stored beyond a small cache.
